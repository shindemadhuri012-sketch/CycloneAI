# Research Literature & Scientific References (`docs/research/`)

## Scientific Foundations
1. **Dvorak, V. F. (1984)**: *Tropical Cyclone Intensity Analysis and Forecasting from Satellite Imagery*. NOAA Technical Report NESDIS 11.
2. **Pradhan, R. et al. (2018)**: *Tropical Cyclone Intensity Estimation Using Deep Convolutional Neural Network*. IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing.
3. **Chen, B. F. et al. (2019)**: *Estimating Tropical Cyclone Intensity by Satellite Imagery Utilizing Convolutional Neural Networks*. Weather and Forecasting.
4. **IMD Cyclone Manual**: *Standard Operating Procedure for Cyclone Warning in India*, India Meteorological Department, Ministry of Earth Sciences.
5. **Selvaraj, R. R. et al. (2020)**: *Deep learning based track and intensity prediction of tropical cyclones in the North Indian Ocean*. Natural Hazards.

## Scientific Objective
To develop an objective, data-driven AI assistant that supplements operational meteorologists during critical early stages of cyclone formation, reducing forecast uncertainty and latency.
