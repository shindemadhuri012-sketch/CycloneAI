# Authoritative Data Sources & Scientific Citations

All data utilized in CycloneAI originates from peer-reviewed scientific repositories and operational meteorological agencies.

---

## 1. NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI)

### Datasets Provided:
- **IBTrACS (International Best Track Archive for Climate Stewardship)** v04r01
- **GridSat-B1** Geostationary Satellite Climate Data Record v02

### Official URL:
- Landing Page: https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/international-best-track-archive
- Direct Access Directory: https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/data/international-best-track-archive-for-climate-stewardship-ibtracs/v04r01/access/

### Formal Academic Citation:
> Knapp, K. R., H. J. Diamond, J. P. Kossin, M. C. Kruk, C. J. Schreck, 2010: *International Best Track Archive for Climate Stewardship (IBTrACS) Project, Version 4.01*. Bulletin of the American Meteorological Society, 91, 363-376. DOI: [10.1175/2009BAMS2755.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/2009BAMS2755.1).

## 2. India Meteorological Department (IMD) / RSMC New Delhi

### Role:
Regional Specialized Meteorological Centre (RSMC) designated by the World Meteorological Organization (WMO) for tropical cyclone advisories in the North Indian Ocean basin (Bay of Bengal and Arabian Sea).

### Datasets Provided:
- Official best-track cyclone reports, central pressures, 3-minute sustained wind estimates, and damage surveys.
- Integrated directly into NOAA IBTrACS under the agency prefix `NEWDELHI_*`.

### Official URL:
- Portal: https://rsmcnewdelhi.imd.gov.in
- Annual Cyclone Reports: https://rsmcnewdelhi.imd.gov.in/report.php?internal_type=NDQ=

### Formal Citation:
> India Meteorological Department (IMD), Ministry of Earth Sciences, Government of India: *Reports on Cyclonic Disturbances over the North Indian Ocean*. RSMC - Tropical Cyclones, New Delhi.

## 4. ISRO Meteorological & Oceanographic Satellite Data Archival Centre (MOSDAC)

### Role:
Primary operational ground segment and archival repository for Indian geostationary earth observation satellites (INSAT-3D, INSAT-3DR, Kalpana-1).

### Datasets Provided:
- Standard Imager Level-1B and Level-1C Calibrated and Geo-referenced Radiances (`3SIMG_L1B_STD`, `3RIMG_L1B_STD`).
- Rapid-scan and sector images over the Indian subcontinent.

### Official URL:
- Portal: https://www.mosdac.gov.in
- Data Catalog: https://www.mosdac.gov.in/catalog/satellite.php

### Access Notes:
Requires free user registration and manual or token-authenticated ordering through the MOSDAC web application.
